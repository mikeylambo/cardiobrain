CardioBrain

Privacy

CardioBrain stores your results only on your device. No accounts, no tracking, no data leaves the app.

What is stored

Your session results, your difficulty levels and your settings, in your browser's storage (or the app's storage on iPhone and Android). Nothing is sent to a server.

What is not collected

No name, email, location, contacts, advertising identifiers or analytics. Nothing is sent to CardioBrain, because there is no CardioBrain server. There is no account to create.

Your control

Settings has Export data, which saves everything as a file, and Delete data, which removes it all from this device. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data also removes it.

Voice answers and spoken prompts

Both are off unless you turn them on. Spoken prompts use your device's own voice. Voice answers use your device's or browser's speech recognition. In the iPhone and Android apps this runs through the system recogniser; in some browsers, notably Chrome, the browser maker processes the audio on its servers under its own privacy policy. CardioBrain never receives, records or stores audio, only whether the words matched an answer.

Heart rate and Health

If you connect a heart-rate strap, its readings stay on this device and are saved with your sessions. In the iPhone and Android apps you can choose to write sessions to Apple Health or Health Connect as mindful minutes; that data then lives in Health under your control.

Sharing

A result is only shared when you tap Share, and it goes wherever you choose in your device's share sheet.
