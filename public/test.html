CardioBrain

Tester guide

Thanks for testing. CardioBrain is quick brain challenges you play while you walk, bike, climb or run. About 30 minutes over a few days is plenty.

Open CardioBrain

1. Install it

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open cardiobrain.vercel.app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from the new icon. It works offline after the first open.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open cardiobrain.vercel.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install when offered, or the menu (⋮), then Add to Home screen.

No account is needed and nothing you do leaves your phone unless you share it.

2. Try these

First run, about 2 minutes

Pick your activity, answer the two questions and play the 30-second round. Was anything unclear? Did you know what to do next?

A real session while moving, 10 minutes

On a walk, bike or treadmill, press Start. Pause once partway through, then finish. Could you read and tap comfortably? Did the difficulty feel right after the first minute?

Listening, 5 minutes

With earbuds, turn on Read aloud (Settings, or the switch on the pause screen) and play while glancing as little as you can. Did the voice keep up? Was the summary at the end useful?

The Daily, 3 minutes

Play today's Daily from Home, then Share and Send a challenge link to someone. Did the link open the same challenge for them?

Seated baseline, 10 minutes

Play one mode sitting down (Change, then Seated), then the same mode while moving. Look at Results and Insights. Did the comparison make sense?

3. Report what you find

  1. Open Settings, scroll to About and tap Report a problem.
  2. Write what happened in a sentence or two.
  3. Tap Share report (or Copy) and send it to the person who invited you.

The report includes the app version, your device, your settings, your last session and any errors the app recorded. You can read all of it before sending. Screenshots help too.

Most useful to hear about

  • Anything that broke, froze or looked wrong.
  • Moments you didn't know what to do.
  • Text or buttons that were hard to read or hit while moving.
  • Challenges that felt far too easy or too hard.
  • What you'd want next.

Check Settings, About for the version you're on. When an update is ready the app says so on Home.
