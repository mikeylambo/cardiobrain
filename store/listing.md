## Description (4,000 max)
CardioBrain gives your mind a workout while your body gets one.

Start a walk, a ride, a stair session or a run, set your phone where you can see it, and answer quick brain challenges as you move. Each one takes a glance and a tap. The screen fills with one bold color, the challenge is set huge, and the answers are big enough to hit mid-stride.

Eight ways to play
• Numbers: quick sums with near-miss answers that keep you honest.
• Switch: odd or even, under or over. The rule changes when you least expect it.
• React: tap where the circle lands. Hold when you see a square.
• Recall: watch a sequence of shapes, then repeat it.
• Rhyme: find the word that rhymes, the closest sound, the odd one out or the next link in a rhyme chain. Built on a real pronunciation dictionary, so it tests your ear, not your spelling.
• N-back: is this letter the same as the one a few back? The classic working-memory task.
• Estimate: a field of dots flashes. About how many?
• Rotate: same shape turned, or its mirror image?
• Mix: the modes you choose, in 75-second blocks. Plus a Daily challenge that's the same for everyone.

It meets you where you are
Difficulty adapts as you play, across 20 levels per mode. Stairs and running get more time to answer than walking. Pick Gentle, Standard or Hard.

Built for moving
• Huge type and tiles, readable in direct sun, upright or sideways on a treadmill.
• Challenges read aloud through your earbuds, and answers by voice, if you like.
• Interval sessions: challenges during your work bouts, or during recovery.
• Connect a Bluetooth heart-rate strap and harder zones get more time to answer.
• Sound and haptics on every answer, so you don't have to stare.
• The screen stays on during a session.
• Leave the app and the session pauses itself. Your progress is saved.
• No typing, ever.

See yourself improve
Play once seated to set a baseline, and CardioBrain shows what moving costs your thinking. Results compare each session with your last one of the same kind: accuracy, response time, best streak and, for Switch, how much a rule change slows you down. History shows your trend for each mode and your last five weeks at a glance. Share a result card when you're proud of one.

Private by design
CardioBrain works fully offline. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking. Your results stay on your phone, and you can export or delete them any time.

Please play safely. Keep your eyes on the road and stay aware of your surroundings. CardioBrain is meant for treadmills, stationary bikes, stair climbers and safe, familiar routes.

## App Review notes
CardioBrain is fully offline and has no sign-in, accounts, purchases or server. All content ships inside the app, including the pronunciation word list used by the Rhyme mode.
To review: tap an activity on the first screen. A 30-second guided round starts after a 3-2-1 countdown. Tap answers on the light tiles at the bottom. Results follow, then Home. Start runs a session with the settings shown; Change picks activity, mode and length.
Haptics use the device's taptic engine. The screen is kept awake only while a session runs. No permissions are requested.
